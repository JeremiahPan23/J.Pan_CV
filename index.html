Ongoing Research Projects

Data-Free Generative Sampling of the Two-Dimensional XY Model

Ongoing

Undergraduate Researcher · The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen · Supervised by Prof. ZHOU Kai

Adapting Stochastic Path Sampling (SPS) to the XY model without pre-generated equilibrium training data, and evaluating physical statistics, sampling efficiency, and rejection behavior in a small-lattice study.

  • Method adaptation: Contributed to energy-based SPS for periodic XY angles, evaluated with path-space independence Metropolis–Hastings (MH) correction.
  • Reproducible experiments: Completed a training-budget comparison across three seeds and three generation-step settings, using Wolff sampling as an independent validation reference.
  • Reliability analysis: Found consistent early efficiency gains and seed-dependent later changes; examined physical statistics and prolonged rejection streaks beyond acceptance rate alone.

Status: Training-budget experiment complete; analysis and report preparation underway.

Statistical Physics Generative Sampling Python PyTorch Slurm
Methods, findings & scope

Methods

Earlier exploration used supervised Flow Matching trained on Wolff-generated configurations. Current SPS training uses the XY energy and forward/backward path probabilities; Wolff configurations serve only as an independent validation reference. Python and PyTorch experiments run on GPUs through Slurm.

Findings

The study compares three seeds, checkpoints at 5,000, 10,000, and 15,000 parameter updates, and 32, 64, and 128 generation steps. Evaluation covers energy, vortex counts, magnetization, spatial correlations, acceptance, effective sample size per sampling second, and consecutive rejection streaks.

Estimated energy sampling efficiency increased in all nine seed/step settings from 5,000 to 10,000 updates. From 10,000 to 15,000, six improved and three declined. All nine settings at both later checkpoints passed predefined physical-statistics screening criteria, providing supporting evidence rather than proof of complete equilibration. High relative path weights can cause prolonged MH rejection streaks; acceptance rate alone does not determine efficiency.

Scope

The motivation is to investigate critical slowing down near the Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless (BKT) transition. Current experiments cover a 4×4 lattice at one temperature near the expected transition; they do not establish thermodynamic critical behavior, a solution to critical slowing down, universal convergence, or an advantage over Wolff sampling. This is a method-adaptation study; SPS is an existing method.

The display title is provisional and is not a finalized thesis title.

Interpretable Crystal Representations for AI-Assisted Materials Design

Ongoing

HSSRLM · AI for Science

Exploring compact crystal descriptors as an interpretable interface between natural language and porous materials, with an emphasis on data provenance, rule-based verification, and reproducible evaluation.

Status: Framework implementation and targeted reliability audits completed; broader dataset validation and model evaluation remain in progress.

AI for Science Crystal Descriptors Rule-Based Verification Python PyTorch Hugging Face Transformers
Framework, verification & scope

Project framework

HSSR (Hierarchical Structure-Sequence Representation) organizes composition, lattice parameters, symmetry, topology, building units, and selected pore-related properties into structured text for metal–organic frameworks (MOFs) and zeolites. The repository implements a T5 (Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer) framework for bidirectional translation between natural language and descriptors; broader model evaluation remains in progress.

Reliability work

The framework includes evidence-aware Crystallographic Information File (CIF)-to-descriptor preprocessing that distinguishes known, inferred, and missing information, preserves provenance, and routes incomplete records for review. Candidate screening checks descriptor format and node/linker/topology connectivity, reports failures explicitly, and tracks duplicates at the descriptor level.

Reproducible structure-equivalence audits examine equivalent representations and information lost by coarse descriptors in three publicly sourced MOF structures. An expanded 26-record metal-node registry addresses ambiguous aliases and preserves source material for continued chemical validation.

Scope

HSSR outputs are material descriptors, not complete atomistic structures in Crystallographic Information File (CIF) format: they lack complete atomic coordinates and a full bonding graph. Verification indicates compatibility with implemented rules, rather than proof of physical validity or synthesizability. The three-structure audit is a limited study and does not establish universal representation invariance.

CrysSpec (Crystal Specification) is a provisional working name.
