\section{Ongoing Research}
\cventry{Ongoing}{Data-Free Generative Sampling of the Two-Dimensional XY Model}{Undergraduate Thesis, CUHK-Shenzhen}{}{}{Supervised by Prof. Kai Zhou. Provisional portfolio title, not a finalized thesis title.
\begin{itemize}
\item Contributed to adapting Stochastic Path Sampling (SPS) to periodic XY angle variables. Training uses the XY energy and forward/backward path probabilities without pre-generated equilibrium configurations as training data. Generated paths are evaluated with path-space independence Metropolis--Hastings (MH) correction.
\item Earlier exploration used supervised Flow Matching trained on Wolff-generated configurations. Wolff sampling is an independent validation reference for the current method.
\item Completed a training-budget comparison across three seeds, 5,000/10,000/15,000 parameter updates, and 32/64/128 generation steps. Python/PyTorch GPU experiments run through Slurm.
\item Evaluated energy, vortex counts, magnetization, spatial correlations, acceptance, effective sample size per sampling second, and consecutive rejection streaks.
\item Estimated energy sampling efficiency increased in all nine seed/step settings from 5,000 to 10,000 updates; from 10,000 to 15,000, six improved and three declined. All nine settings at both later checkpoints passed predefined physical-statistics screening criteria; this supports, but does not prove, complete equilibration.
\item High relative path weights can cause prolonged MH rejection streaks. Acceptance alone does not determine sampling efficiency. Analysis and report preparation are underway.
\end{itemize}The limited $4\times4$ lattice study at one temperature near the expected Berezinskii--Kosterlitz--Thouless (BKT) transition does not establish thermodynamic critical behavior, a solution to critical slowing down, universal convergence, or an advantage over Wolff. SPS is an existing method.}

\cventry{Ongoing}{Interpretable Crystal Representations for AI-Assisted Materials Design}{HSSRLM --- AI for Science --- Working paper}{}{}{An initial AAAI submission plan was deferred while another manuscript took priority. Possible future venues include IJCAI and ACL, subject to fit and further development. A collaborator indicated that my current contributions could support co-first authorship; co-first, second, or other authorship positions depend on final contributions and author agreement. Submission plans and final author order remain unconfirmed. Framework: compact hierarchical descriptors form an interpretable interface between natural language and porous materials, including metal--organic frameworks (MOFs) and zeolites.
\begin{itemize}
\item Hierarchical Structure-Sequence Representation (HSSR) organizes composition, lattice parameters, symmetry, topology, building units, and selected pore-related properties into structured text. The repository implements T5 (Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer) infrastructure for bidirectional text/descriptor translation.
\item Contributed to evidence-aware Crystallographic Information File (CIF)-to-descriptor preprocessing that separates known, inferred, and missing information, preserves provenance, and routes incomplete records for review.
\item Contributed to multi-candidate screening with descriptor validation, node/linker/topology connectivity checks, explicit failure reporting, and descriptor-level duplicate tracking.
\item Contributed to reproducible structure-equivalence audits on three publicly sourced MOF structures, examining equivalent representations and information lost by coarse descriptors. This is a limited study, not evidence of universal representation invariance.
\end{itemize}Framework implementation and targeted reliability audits are complete; broader dataset validation and model evaluation remain in progress. HSSR omits complete atomic coordinates and a full bonding graph, so its outputs are descriptors rather than complete atomistic CIF structures. Verification establishes compatibility with implemented rules, not physical validity or synthesizability. CrysSpec (Crystal Specification) is a provisional working name. The framework also includes a 26-record metal-node registry that addresses ambiguous aliases and preserves source material for continued chemical validation.}
