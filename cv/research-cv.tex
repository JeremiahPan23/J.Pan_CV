\section{Current Research}
\cventry{Ongoing}{Data-Free Generative Sampling of the Two-Dimensional XY Model}{Undergraduate Thesis, CUHK-Shenzhen}{}{}{Supervised by Prof. Kai Zhou. Provisional portfolio title.
\begin{itemize}
\item Contributed to adapting Stochastic Path Sampling (SPS) to periodic XY angles using energy and forward/backward path probabilities without equilibrium training data; evaluated paths with path-space independence Metropolis--Hastings (MH) correction.
\item Completed reproducible Python/PyTorch training-budget experiments across three seeds and three generation-step settings on Slurm-managed GPUs; used Wolff sampling as an independent validation reference.
\item Evaluated physical statistics, effective sample size per sampling second, and rejection streaks; found consistent early gains and seed-dependent later changes. Scope: $4\times4$, one temperature; no established thermodynamic critical behavior or advantage over Wolff.
\end{itemize}}
\cventry{Ongoing}{Interpretable Crystal Representations for AI-Assisted Materials Design}{HSSRLM --- AI for Science --- Working paper}{}{}{Hierarchical crystal descriptors with T5 (Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer) bidirectional text/descriptor infrastructure; broader data validation and model evaluation ongoing.
\begin{itemize}
\item Contributed to evidence-aware CIF (Crystallographic Information File)-to-descriptor preprocessing preserving provenance and known/inferred/missing information.
\item Contributed to candidate validation, node/linker/topology connectivity checks, explicit failure reporting, and descriptor-level duplicate tracking.
\item Contributed to limited structure-equivalence audits on three publicly sourced metal--organic frameworks, examining information lost by coarse descriptors.
\end{itemize}Descriptors omit full atomistic structures; rule checks do not prove physical validity. Co-first author; manuscript in preparation for planned submission to the Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL). Not yet submitted.}
