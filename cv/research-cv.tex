\section{Current Research}
\cventry{Ongoing}{Data-Free Generative Sampling of the Two-Dimensional XY Model}{Undergraduate Researcher, CUHK-Shenzhen}{}{}{Supervised by Prof. Kai Zhou. Provisional portfolio title.
\begin{itemize}
\item Contributed to adapting Stochastic Path Sampling (SPS) to periodic XY angles, using energy and forward/backward path probabilities without pre-generated equilibrium training configurations; evaluated paths with path-space independence Metropolis--Hastings (MH) correction.
\item Completed a reproducible training-budget experiment across three seeds and three generation-step settings in Python/PyTorch on Slurm-managed GPUs; Wolff sampling provided an independent validation reference.
\item Evaluated physical statistics, effective sample size per sampling second, and rejection streaks. Early efficiency gains were consistent; later changes were seed dependent. The limited $4\times4$, single-temperature study does not establish thermodynamic critical behavior or an advantage over Wolff.
\end{itemize}}
